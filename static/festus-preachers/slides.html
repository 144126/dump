RETRACING THE SCRIPTURAL POSITION OF THE PREACHER AND THE CHURCH

TEXT: JEREMIAH 6:16, 1 CORINTHIANS 3:6-9

01

INTRODUCTION

02

I beseech us by the Mercy and Love of Christ that we journey together through the scripture with regards to the topic, let us bear in mind that all we are going to be discussing would be to the Glory of God and for the edification of the Church, Being likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, and of one mind (Phil. 2:2).

It is important to acknowledge that the Church is a family related by the Blood of Christ wherein Christ is the Head of the Body the Church (Col.1:18) and the Husband of the Bride the Church, Hence the Church is not a business enterprise or company or Government organization, it is a divine relationship and a testimony of the love of God as every member of the family has a role to play for growth and expansion. (1Cor.12:12-20, Eph.4:16)

AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

03
  • To understand the relationship between the preacher and the Church
  • To understand the scriptural Duties, roles and functions of a preacher
  • To understand the scriptural Duties of the Church towards the preacher and as a Church for growth
  • To work together as one body for the Glory of God and edification of the Church
  • To learn and to implement

DEFINITION

04

Retracing the scriptural position of the Preacher and the Church: In simple terms is returning to the scripture to recover the New Testament pattern of the relationship between the Preacher and the Church and how they are meant to function to the Glory of God and for the growth of the Church.

Jeremiah 6:16 "Thus says the Lord: Stand in the ways and see, And ask for the old paths, where the good way is, And walk in it; Then you will find rest for your souls."

1Cor.3:8-9 "Now he who plants and he who waters are one, and each one will receive his own reward according to his own labor. For we are God's fellow workers; you are God's field, you are God's building."

THE RELATIONSHIP

05

The relationship between the Preacher, the elders, deacons and members as the Church, is called FELLOWSHIP IN THE GOSPEL (Phil.1:5)

Phil.1:5 "for your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now,"

FELLOWSHIP IN THE GOSPEL: Is the shared partnership of brethren working together to advance the Gospel of Christ, each member contributing through preaching, teaching, upholding gospel workers, prayers, sacrificial giving, and a life of faithfulness. It functions as a team, where the preacher fulfill his duties and the church fulfills hers, each complementing the other rather than working in isolation. This partnership does not operate from organizational structure, because the church is a family related by the Blood of Christ, bond together by love and mutual responsibility.

Eph.4:16 "from whom the whole body, joined and knit together by what EVERY JOINT SUPPLIES, according to the effective working by which EVERY PART DOES IT SHARE, causes growth of the body for the edifying of itself in love."

WHO IS A PREACHER, AN EVANGELIST, AND A MINISTER?

06
  • A preacher is one called and sent by God to proclaim the gospel of Christ, to teach the word of God and not himself. 1Tim.2:7, Rom.10:14-15, 1Cor.15:1-4, 2Cor.4:5.
  • An Evangelist is a Minister who is called by God to go about preaching the Gospel to the lost. Acts 8:4-5, Acts 21:8
  • A Minister is simply a Servant, one who serves God Faithfully in the Gospel of Christ. 1Cor.4:1-2

NOTE: All are Ministers but all are not Teachers, Evangelists, Preachers, Deacons and Elders rather all are called to preaching and evangelism and ministering. Eph.4:11, Rom.12:6-8, 1Pet.4:10-11, Mathew 28:19-20, James 3:1

To preach is to proclaim: Repent for the Kingdom of God is at hand

To teach is to instruct: Explaining what Repent and the Kingdom means for adequate understanding

The Apostle: Preached and taught daily in the temple and every house Acts 5:42

Paul ordained A Preacher, Apostle, and a Teacher 1Tim.2:7, 2Tim.1:11

Therefore A Preacher is a Minister (1Cor.4:1, 1Cor.3:5), Evangelist and a Teacher.

THE PURPOSE (WHY) OF THE CALL

08

God in His infinite wisdom has called people into ministry that, through faithful preaching of the Gospel; the lost souls are saved, (Rom.1:16), to preach the unsearchable riches of Christ (Eph.3:6-8), to reveal the mystery in Christ (Col.1:26-27, Eph.3:3-4), equipping the saints for the work of ministry and edifying the Church of Christ (Eph.4:12), and to preserve the sound doctrine (Eph.4:14).

THE QUALITIES OF A MINISTER

09

The qualities are the Godly characters and abilities of a Minister

  • Faithful: He is trustworthy and reliable to deliver what he has received 1Cor.4:2
  • Apt to teach, gentle, patience and not quarrelsome, 2Tim.2:24-25
  • Showing example and pattern, 1Tim.4:12, 1Cor.11:1, Titus 2:7, Phil.4:9.
  • Willingness, Readiness, and desire to preach at all times, Rom.1:15, 1Cor.9:16-17, 1Thess.2:8-9

  • Studying the word, 2Tim.2:15
  • Not self commending, 2Cor.10:12-18, 2Cor.4:5.
  • Of Good Report: Acts 16:1-2

THE AUTHORITY OF A MINISTER

10

The Minister who has been called by God also has been instructed by God in the doctrine of Christ and by the inspiration of God given as the scripture which is the standard and the manual for his Roles, functions and duties wherein he has the right to instruct, teach, command, rebuke, reproof, and correct for the edification of the Church in the way of the Lord and not self imposed authority. 2Cor.10:8, 2Cor.13:10, 1Tim.4:6-11 (these things command and teach), 2Tim.3:16-17.

ROLES, FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES

11

WHO HE IS · WHAT HE DOES · WHAT HE MUST DO

It is important for the Church and every Christian to know the Role, Functions, Duties, and acknowledge Ministers have been given the authority to function in these capacities by God and not one who decides of himself as it pleases him but as one who plays his part in the kingdom of God as his Spiritual Service (Eph.4:16) which include

  • EVANGELISM: 2Tim.4:5
  • TESTIFY OF THE GOSPEL: Rom.1:16, 2Tim.1:8, Acts.14:26-27, Acts.15:2,12, Acts.20:18-24, Acts.11:4
  • PREACHING (Acts.8:5-12, Col.1:28-29, 1Tim.2:7) and TEACHING (Acts.5:42, Acts.15:35, 2Tim.1:11)
  • TEACHING THE WHOLE COUNSEL OF GOD: Acts.20:20-27, The preacher delivers as he has received (1Cor.15:1-3, 1Cor.14:37) withholding nothing, without compromise, with no favouritism or partiality (1Tim.5:21), or self Glorifying, selective self-pleasing topics and pleasing men (Ga.1:10, 1Thess.2:4-6)
  • ASSESSMENT OF SPIRITUAL GROWTH: The Minister who impart the saints also assess the spiritual growth and maturity of the Church (Act 15:36), and for equipping of the saints and for the work of ministry (Eph.4:12)

ROLES, FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES (CONT'D)

12

ROLES, FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES (CONT'D)

  • DISCIPLESHIP (Commit unto faithful men, 2Tim.2:2): The Faithful Minister also commit unto faithful men for the continuity and advancement of the Gospel, whereby these men could be sent to go further sow in the field, also to set things in order.

Titus left in Crete (Titus 1:5)

Are there Faithful men to be discipled?

  • APPOINTMENT OF ELDERS/DEACONS: The existence of elders in the Church as the Spiritual leadership comes from consistent teaching which leads to maturity, increases knowledge, Spirituality, desire, qualification, and ordination for sustaining edification, and sound doctrine and not a political office in the Church but by scriptural authorize qualifications (Acts.14:21-23, Titus.1:5, 1Tim.3:14-15), not hastily laying of hands without scriptural examination (1Tim.5:22), and testing (1Tim.3:10), to watch over the saints and sustaining sound doctrine.

The Ministers have been called by God and given manual of operation as Minister(s), the Church also has been giving command on her Roles/duties toward the Minister and as a Church.

  • ACKNOWLEDGE (1Cor.4:1): The Scriptural command to the individual Christian and the Church is to regard, and acknowledge them (preachers) that have been called by God as Ministers of Christ and Stewards of the mysteries of God.
  • RECOGNISE AND ESTEEM (1Thess.5:12-13): It is important to Recognise (know) not only by name but by the work which God has given the Ministers (Preachers, Elders, Deacons) who are over the Church as their Spiritual Service, labouring diligently and for your own growth (Acts 20:19-20,31) and ESTEEM THEM VERY HIGHLY (Honor, Respect, value and regard)

METHOD/ PROCESS OF PREACHER ENGAGEMENT

20

This has been a topic of concern and how it affects the growth of the Church, hence retracing the scriptural position of the Preacher and the Church exposes whether the Preacher is the employee of the Church and the Church as his employer. The Scripture provides clarity on the relationship between the Preacher and the Church for the Glory of God who has made Jesus the Head of the Church and the Church His Body.

The relationship between the Preacher and the Church, is called FELLOWSHIP IN THE GOSPEL (Phil.1:5)

Phil.1:5 "for your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now,"

FELLOWSHIP IN THE GOSPEL: Is the shared partnership of brethren working together to advance the Gospel of Christ, each member contributing through preaching, teaching, upholding gospel workers, prayer, sacrificial giving, and a life of faithfulness. (Eph.4:16, "every joint supplies and every part does its share") It functions as a team, where the preacher fulfill his duties and the church fulfills hers, each complementing the other rather than working in isolation. This partnership does not operate from organizational structure, because the church is a family related by the Blood of Christ, bond together by love and mutual responsibility.

THE MINDSET/ TRADITION

21

It is important that we renew our minds, that we (may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God. Rom.12:2), so that we do not (by tradition make the command of God of no effect. Math.15:6)

Therefore, the Preacher is not the employee of the Church rather one who is called by God and so works for God because he is to give account to God for the responsibility that has been given to him (Heb.13:17), hence the mindset or tradition that the Preacher is employed by the Church base on certain listed qualifications which he MUST have or possessed to do the work of a Preacher does not sit anywhere in the scripture and is not the will of God concerning the edification of the Church and propagation of the Gospel.

  • Authority to Employ: The scripture does not authorize the Church either by direct command, Apostolic example or by necessary inference to employ a Preacher, rather by direct command, and examples instruct the Church to support the one who already is a Preacher (1Cor.9:14, Gal.6:6, 2Cor.11:8-9, Phil.4:15-18, 3John 1:5-8), and to esteem them highly in love for their work's sake (1Thess.5:13).
  • Validation: Supporting a Preacher does not make him or validate him a preacher, he is a preacher by the work, necessity and stewardship which brings about the support and not payment, 1Cor.9:14,16-17. Therefore supporting the preacher materially or financially is based on the congregational arrangements that is expedient for the work, but the command must be carried out.

22

The phrase, Evangelist/Minister/Preacher of this/our/their/his/her/my congregation is only but a phrase to identify Location and Recipient of the teachings of the Minister at the particular time and not ownership of a restricted employee/staff of a single congregation, the Preacher belongs to God since he is called, sent, and given the message by God to deliver, therefore he works for God while serving with the Church and not working for the church as employee, hence the preacher does not belong to the Church neither does the Church belongs to him and he preaches round the world, Rom.15:20 for we belong to God and the Church is one, 1Cor.1:12-13.

Examples: OF = The Gentiles were recipients of Paul's teaching, 1Tim.2:7, 2Tim.1:11

Servants of Christ, 1Cor.4:1

Timothy Minister of God, 1Thess.3:2

Paul servant of God/Christ. Titus 1:1, Rom.1:1. e.t.c

Therefore getting rid of such tradition/mindset that the Minister is paid/employed to do the work strengthens the unity of the Church in the fellowship of the Gospel, than sustaining complacency.

MINISTER'S RETIREMENT

25

The New Testament does not provide any command or example that a Minister is pronounced retired by the Church as a result of age, or drafted condition of service or public service rule or signed contract, since he is not the employee of the Church but Minister of God.

Whereas a Minister is old in age, unable to walk about preaching he is still sound in faith and doctrine committing onto faithful men who would commit onto others, he is not to be retired by Church except by death.
